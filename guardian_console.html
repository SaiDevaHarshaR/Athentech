Quick recovery playbooks

Plain-language first steps

◉   Excel results show zero

Inspect payment metric and source columns. Compare against read-only SQL totals before relying on reports.

◉   AI requests fail

Check provider availability, account limits and error category. Instant reports may still work.

◉   Client database offline

Run self-check. Confirm SQL connection details and connectivity without editing client data.

◉   PDF export fails

Inspect report generation errors, browser renderer and disk capacity.

For unknown errors, download the sanitized support package and escalate. Guardian does not repair or modify client LIS tables.
